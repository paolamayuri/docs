## Concept

With Z-API, you can now make calls to any number, including sending audio during the call. For more details on how to use this feature, check the next pages in the documentation.

Additionally, you can use the feature known as "call attention" — similar to the old Facebook “poke”. With it, you can start a call, wait up to 15 seconds, and automatically hang up.

Through message webhooks, you can track call events and identify whether the recipient answered or rejected the call.

## About calls

The call instance can also send messages — meaning that once you subscribe to a call instance, you can use that same instance to make up to 3 simultaneous calls and send messages.

While on a call, messages continue to be sent normally, with no interruption to the queue.
